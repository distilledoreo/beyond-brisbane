How accurate are these prices?

Airfare is sourced from Google Flights via SerpApi and cached for up to three days (200 searches/month cap). It is the lowest separate one-way economy fare for one adult; six seats at that rate are not guaranteed. Airport timing, baggage, connections and through-ticket availability must be checked before booking. Aside from the booked Brisbane hotel, ground costs are November 2026 research-based estimates, not July 2027 quotes. The planning range is a scenario, not a statistical confidence interval. Meal/tour choices, actual bed configuration and exchange rates can materially change totals.

How the estimates and room-sharing comparisons are calculated

Accommodation: Brisbane is confirmed at A$10,700 for six, converted at A$1 = US$0.701513 on November 4, 2026. Each person's share is about US$1,251. For other stays, we compare three rooms (including a 12% tax/fee estimate) with a legal six-person rental including cleaning fees. We prefer hotel rooms if the rental saves less than 5%. These other rates are shopping targets, not verified July 2027 bookings.

Other costs: Destination-based daily meal and local travel budgets plus itinerary-specific excursions, airport transfers, insurance, checked baggage, travel-day meals and entry allowances. Add a 10% buffer to unbooked ground expenses, excluding the confirmed Brisbane hotel. Flights retain the live-feed cached price; the displayed scenario range assumes ground costs approximately 15% lower to 30% higher and fares up to 40% higher than the lowest returned separate legs.

Research benchmarks: Brisbane · Sydney · Fiji · Queenstown · 3-bedroom Bali villas · Cairns · Tokyo · Singapore · Darwin · Vietnam · Melbourne · Six-person accommodation comparison · Singapore rental restrictions.

Other accommodation and cost benchmarks collected November 4, 2026. Brisbane lodging is booked; its USD equivalent uses that date's reference rate. Other July 2027 room availability and prices are not verified. Some itinerary narrative transfer dates differ from the flight-search dates; the cost model follows the flight-search legs.
